Biological Justification

NRF2 is activated by oxidative/nitrosative stress via KEAP1 cysteine oxidation. In cutaneous leishmaniasis, parasite-triggered ROS release NRF2 from KEAP1, driving antioxidant gene expression.

+SOD — Direct NRF2/ARE target gene (SOD1/SOD2). Strongest marker: ×32.0 in patients. Confirms active NRF2 nuclear translocation.
−GSH, −Catalase — GSH depletion maintains KEAP1 in an oxidized state, sustaining NRF2 nuclear residence. Low Catalase reflects H₂O₂ overload that exceeds the antioxidant capacity, amplifying the NRF2 activation signal.
+MPO, +NO — ROS/RNS generators that oxidize/nitrosate KEAP1, triggering NRF2 release.
−Arginase — Competes with iNOS for L-arginine, partially blunting the nitrosative NRF2 signal.
